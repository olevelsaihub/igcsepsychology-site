Idea. Why are you good at what you are good at? Part is built into you, and part comes from what happens to you. Nature means inherited, biological influences, like genes and brain chemistry. Nurture means influences from outside, like family, friends, culture and life experiences. Here is how to sort causes of depression and addiction. A parent with the same problem points to genes, so nature. D R D two is a gene linked to addiction, and five H T T is linked to depression. Stressful events, friends praising you, copying a role model, or learning negative thinking all come from outside, so nurture. Most psychologists say it is not one or the other. Genes may make someone vulnerable. The environment may then trigger the problem. Caspi found exactly this, a gene plus stressful events. A question may ask you to define a term, describe the difference, or pick a cause in a scenario and name it nature or nurture. Define questions want one sentence and an example. Always match the right term.

Method. Step one: decide if the question wants nature, nurture, or the difference. Step two: define the term in one sentence, inherited or environmental. Step three: pick a matching detail from the scenario, such as a parent with the same problem for nature, or friends and stress for nurture. The traps are calling a parent's influence through copying nature, when it is nurture, and defining a term with no example when one is asked for. In a difference question, describing only one side loses a mark. Use the word whereas to contrast them.

Worked example. Here is a real exam question. Dawood's friends keep praising his shoes. He ends up with an addiction to shoes. We explain one way nurture could explain it. Two marks. Step one: define nurture. Nurture means influences from the environment, such as other people and experiences, not inherited factors. Step two: pick a detail from the scenario. Dawood's friends kept complimenting his shoes each time he bought some. That rewarded his buying, and encouraged him to buy more and more. A tempting wrong route is to say his brother also buys shoes, so Dawood inherited it. That calls copying nature. Swapping shoes with his brother is environment too. Reject the genetic story. The exam answer. Define, then apply. One mark for understanding nurture. One mark for applying it to the friends' praise. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. The most common mistake is calling copying a parent nature. Nature is only what is inherited. If a person learned it by watching, it is nurture.
