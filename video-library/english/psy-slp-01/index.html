Idea. After a night studying until late, you feel foggy and slow at school. After a good night's sleep you feel sharp. Sleep is not wasted time. Your body and brain are busy while you sleep. Sleep is not one state. It moves in sleep cycles. One cycle lasts about ninety minutes, and it repeats four or five times a night. Each cycle has four stages of non-REM sleep, then REM sleep. REM stands for rapid eye movement. In REM the eyes dart about and the brain is very active. The heart rate rises. But the body muscles are paralysed, so the body does not move. Most vivid dreaming happens in REM. Why does sleep matter? Deep sleep releases growth hormone, which repairs the body. Sleep saves energy. It helps the brain consolidate memories, which means store them firmly. And it keeps attention, concentration and mood steady. Method. Step one: state the stage feature or benefit accurately. That earns the first mark. Step two: link it to the person or what they did in the scenario. That earns the second mark. The trap is a vague answer, such as sleep is good for you, with no link. For stage questions, match the clue to the stage. Spindles mean stage two. Delta waves mean stages three and four. Darting eyes mean REM.
