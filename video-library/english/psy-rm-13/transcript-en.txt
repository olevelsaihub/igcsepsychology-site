Idea. A class has twelve girls and eighteen boys. A ratio compares two amounts. Girls to boys is twelve to eighteen. We write twelve colon eighteen. The order matters. It follows the words of the question, so girls come first. A ratio should always be simplified. Divide both numbers by the highest common factor. That is the biggest number that divides exactly into both. For twelve and eighteen it is six. So twelve to eighteen simplifies to two to three. Be careful with the words all and the rest. A ratio of girls to all children uses the total as the second number. A ratio of girls to the rest uses the total minus the girls. This question type says: calculate the ratio of one group to another. You find both amounts, write them in order, and simplify.

Method. Step one: work out both amounts, in the order asked. Step two: write them as a colon b. This shows your method. Step three: divide both by the highest common factor, so the ratio is in its simplest form. The trap is the word all. Ratio to all participants uses the total. Many students use the rest instead, and lose the mark.

Worked example. Here is a real exam question. Thomas tests whether a man's clothes affect obedience. We calculate the ratio of participants who disobeyed, to all participants taking part. It is worth two marks. Here are the results. The question asks for those who disobeyed. That is the disobeyed column: eight in Condition A and nineteen in Condition B. Step one. Both amounts. Disobeyed is eight plus nineteen, which is twenty seven. All participants is the whole group, sixty. Step two. Write the ratio in the order asked: disobeyed first, then all. Twenty seven colon sixty. Step three. Simplify. The highest common factor of twenty seven and sixty is three. Twenty seven divided by three is nine. Sixty divided by three is twenty. Nine and twenty share no other factor, so nine colon twenty is fully simplified. A tempting wrong route is disobeyed to obeyed, twenty seven to thirty three. That compares the group to the rest. But the question says to all participants, so the second number must be sixty. Now the exam-ready answer. Disobeyed equals eight plus nineteen, which is twenty seven. All equals sixty. Twenty seven colon sixty simplifies to nine colon twenty. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is to simplify only part of the way. Twelve to thirty can be divided by three to give four to ten. But four and ten still share the factor two. Divide by the highest common factor, six, to reach two to five.

Answer. First find both amounts. A quarter of twenty is twenty divided by four, which is five males. The rest are females: twenty minus five is fifteen.
