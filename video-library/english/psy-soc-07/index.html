Idea. You live in a country, a city, a school. People around you follow certain rules without thinking. A society is a large group of people who live in the same area and share rules, laws and norms of behaviour. Norms are the usual ways people are expected to behave. Keep society, culture and social issue apart, because they are three different terms. Culture is different. Culture means the shared beliefs, values, customs and traditions of a group of people, passed on across generations. A social issue is a problem that affects many people in a society, such as crime or prejudice. Cultures differ. Individualist cultures, such as the USA and the UK, value independence and personal goals. Collectivist cultures, such as China and Japan, value group harmony and duty. Culture can affect behaviour. Bond and Smith, in nineteen ninety-six, found that people from collectivist cultures tend to conform more. Also, much social influence research was done in the USA, so findings may not fit other cultures. These are short questions: define a term, or describe one way culture matters.

Method. Step one: give a precise one-sentence definition with the key idea. Shared place and norms for society. Shared customs and values for culture. Step two: for describe questions, name one effect of culture, then develop it with an individualist and collectivist contrast. Describe means say what it is, with detail. The traps are treating society and culture as the same, and giving only an example instead of a definition.

Worked example. Here is a real exam question. It is short. Define the term society. Define means give the exact meaning. One mark. We write one precise sentence. Step one: recall the key idea. A society is people living together, who share rules and norms. Step two: write it as one sentence. A society is a large group of people living in the same place, who follow a shared set of rules, laws and expected ways of behaving. A tempting wrong route is to define society as culture, shared beliefs and customs. Those are two different terms, and mixing them up loses the mark. Society is about shared rules in a shared area. The exam answer. One mark, for a definition with the place and the shared norms. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is to give only an example, like, the United Kingdom. An example is not a definition. Say what the word means.

Answer. Culture can affect conformity. People from collectivist cultures value group harmony, so they tend to conform more. People from individualist cultures value independence, so they tend to conform less. Bond and Smith found this. One mark for the effect, and one for developing it. Keep it short and exact. A common wrong answer is, people in different countries have different cultures. That is true, but it says nothing about behaviour. It does not answer the question. So name one effect of culture, and develop it with a contrast.
