Idea. A pizza is cut into eight slices and you eat two. You ate two out of eight. That is a fraction. A fraction is a part written over the whole. Two is the part, eight is the whole, so the fraction is two eighths. We simplify fractions. Divide the top and bottom by the highest common factor, the biggest number that goes into both. For two and eight it is two. So two eighths becomes one quarter. Lowest form means it cannot be simplified any more. Read the condition carefully. More than ten does not include ten. Take the scores four, eight, ten, twelve and fifteen. More than ten means only twelve and fifteen. This question type says: calculate how many, as a fraction of all. You count the group, find the total, and write the group over the total in its lowest form.

Method. Step one: count how many are in the group described, and check the exact condition. Step two: find the total, which is the whole group. Step three: write the group over the total, then simplify fully. The trap is turning the fraction upside down. The group goes on top. The total goes on the bottom.

Worked example. Here is a real exam question. John tests a new drug for depression. A placebo is a dummy pill with no active drug in it. We calculate how many patients had the placebo, as a fraction of all patients, in its lowest form. It is worth two marks. Here are the groups. Condition A had twenty patients, with the placebo. Condition B had forty, with the drug. All patients is sixty. Step one. The group is the placebo patients: twenty. Step two. The total is all patients: sixty. Step three. Write the group over the total: twenty over sixty. Step four. Simplify. The highest common factor of twenty and sixty is twenty. Twenty divided by twenty is one. Sixty divided by twenty is three. So the fraction is one third. A tempting wrong route is twenty over forty, which is one half. That puts the placebo group over Condition B only. The question says all patients, so the bottom number must be sixty. Now the exam-ready answer. Twenty patients out of sixty received the placebo. Twenty over sixty simplifies to one third. Common mistake. A common mistake is counting the boundary. Take five students who spent fifteen, thirty, fifty, fifty five and eighty minutes. More than thirty minutes does not include thirty. Only three students count.
