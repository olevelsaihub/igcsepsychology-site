Why these types are in the plan
20selected question types
3learning days

The review covered 14 current component papers from 2019–2025.

Current-paper marks were assigned to each part’s sole primary type, and every selected type’s prerequisites were added.

Historical coverage check

5 of 5 current paper pairs cleared the target

The route covered at least 82/177 primary-owned marks on every complete 2019–2025 pair. Each pair stayed at least 10 marks above the higher of its own historical Grade 5 boundary and the matching June 2026 Grade 5 boundary.

This is 100% historical paper-pair coverage, not a 100% probability claim for a future student grade. Accuracy, timing, independent recall and unseen wording still matter.

Completion time

3 learning days plus 6 paper sessions

Study 6 to 7 types per day, then sit three complete Paper 1 + Paper 2 mock pairs as six separate paper sessions. That is 9 active days.

This keeps the olevels.com Pass Plan pace of about 6.6 types per learning day.

Readiness gate

Completion is practice. Fresh papers confirm readiness.

After completing the plan, attempt three fresh, unseen 177-mark Paper 1 + Paper 2 paper pairs under timed conditions. Have each pair independently marked with its official scheme. Grade 5 readiness is supported only when every score is at least 10 marks above that exact pair’s published Grade 5 boundary.

Supporting skills for Psychology

Use these short bridges when a starting skill needs strengthening.

Reading a psychology question and its scenario

Find the command word and the marks, underline the scenario details (who, what happened), and match the answer length and form to the command word.

Using named studies

Recall a study's aim, procedure, results and conclusion, and use one point from it as evidence for or against a theory.
