Before you start: supporting skills

These starting skills support many types. Check them first, and come back to them whenever a type feels hard.

  • Reading a psychology question and its scenario: Find the command word and the marks, underline the scenario details (who, what happened), and match the answer length and form to the command word.
  • Using named studies: Recall a study's aim, procedure, results and conclusion, and use one point from it as evidence for or against a theory.
