Knowledge card

Prison, community sentences and restorative justice

Written

Each punishment is judged by its effect on recidivism (reoffending): prison removes freedom, community sentences keep offenders in society, restorative justice meets the victim.

4

The method

  1. Name and define

    Say what the punishment involves.

  2. Apply it

    What would this offender do or lose?

  3. Link to reoffending

    Say whether it makes recidivism more or less likely.

  4. Judge

    In long answers, weigh protection against reoffending risk.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Community vs restorative

    Community = unpaid work; restorative = meeting the victim.

  • ✗
    Prison as treatment

    Prison punishes; it does not treat causes.

  • ✗
    No offender

    Each point must be about the named person.

Prison, community sentences and restorative justice · June 2023 · Paper 2 · Question 11 · part (whole) · 4 marks

11 Calvin caused a road traffic accident nine months ago by driving dangerously over the speed limit. The court banned him from driving for 12 months and removed his driving licence. Calvin has now caused a second road traffic accident by driving while above the legal alcohol limit and without a licence. He has been sentenced to six months’ imprisonment. Explain one strength and one weakness of prison as a punishment for Calvin’s behaviour. Strength Weakness

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Strength: prison removes Calvin from roads, protecting the public, and shows how serious his offence is.
  2. Justify: he cannot drive while locked up and may be deterred later.
  3. Weakness: prison punishes but does not treat his drinking.
  4. Justify: on release the cause remains, so he may reoffend.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Strength: While Calvin is in prison he is kept away from society and cannot get behind a wheel, so other road users are safe from his dangerous driving for six months. Losing his freedom may also make him realise how serious his crimes are, which could deter him from driving dangerously after release. Weakness: Prison simply punishes Calvin for what he did and does not tackle his drinking, which led to the second accident. When he is released his alcohol problem may still be there, so he could drink and drive again and reoffend.

How the marks are earned

One mark for each identified strength/weakness and one for each linked justification about Calvin's future behaviour.
