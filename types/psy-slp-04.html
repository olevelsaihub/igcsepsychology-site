Zeitgebers, light and Siffre's cave study

Written

Zeitgebers are external cues, mainly light, that reset the body clock. Siffre showed that without them the sleep-wake cycle drifts away from 24 hours.

The method

  1. Find the cue

    Which external cue is present or missing?

  2. Explain the effect

    Show how it resets or upsets the cycle, or use Siffre.

  3. Two ways or assess

    Two findings for 4 marks; evaluate for 9.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Light is external

    Light is a zeitgeber, not an internal factor.

  • ✗
    Exactly 24 hours

    Siffre's cycle drifted widely.

  • ✗
    Single case

    Say it is one man when you evaluate.

33 Leonardo is training to be an astronaut. As part of his training, he spends four months in isolation in a purpose-built soundproof laboratory where there is no natural light. Leonardo is observed during these four months completing tasks to test his memory processing and dexterity. During his time in the laboratory, Leonardo begins to lose track of the days and struggles to know when to sleep. He finds it increasingly difficult to complete tasks, such as finishing a crossword puzzle and sewing buttons onto a shirt. Explain two ways the findings from Siffre (1975) could account for Leonardo’s behaviour. 1 ................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................ 2 ................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................................

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Way 1: no daylight cue, so lost track of days and sleep timing; Siffre misjudged the date.
  2. Way 2: worse fine motor tasks; Siffre's dexterity declined in isolation.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

1 Leonardo loses track of the days and of when to sleep because there is no sunlight to act as an external cue keeping his sleep-wake cycle to 24 hours. Siffre (1975) found that without daylight his own cycle drifted to anything from about 18 to over 50 hours, and when he came out he thought fewer days had passed than really had. 2 Leonardo finds it harder to sew buttons and finish crosswords as time goes on. Siffre reported that his long isolation from external cues was followed by a decline in his memory and in his fine hand control, which matches Leonardo's falling performance.

How the marks are earned

Each way: 1 mark for the point about Leonardo, 1 for the matching Siffre finding.
