CBT as a treatment for depression and addiction

Written

CBT is a talking therapy that challenges irrational thoughts and changes the behaviour linked to them, with homework between sessions.

4

The method

  1. Name a technique

    Challenging thoughts, homework or a thought diary.

  2. Apply it

    Show what it would look like for this person.

  3. Evaluate for them

    Tie a strength or weakness to the person, e.g. motivation.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Vague talk

    "Talking about problems" is not a CBT technique.

  • ✗
    Drugs

    Medication is a different treatment.

  • ✗
    Point repeated

    The justification must add a reason, not restate the point.

Worked explanation

  1. Choose a technique: identifying and challenging irrational thoughts.
  2. Apply it to Jeremie's belief that he must carry on bidding.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

In CBT the therapist helps the client spot irrational thoughts and question them so they can be replaced with more realistic ones. Jeremie's therapist could help him see that his belief that he must keep bidding to feel good is irrational, for example by asking how many of the hundreds of items he actually needs, so that he cuts down his buying.

How the marks are earned

One mark for correctly explaining a CBT technique; one for applying it to Jeremie's auction buying.
