Knowledge card

Spotting the IV and the DV

Written

The IV is what the researcher changes or compares. The DV is what they measure at the end. Anything kept the same for everyone is a control.

1

Which variable is which?

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

3 variables to find

Find the IV, the DV and one control.

  1. IV: silence or music
  2. Control: same time
  3. DV: words recalled

IV: silence or music

Why
This is the only thing that differs between the groups.

Control: same time

Why
Kept the same for everyone, so it is not the IV or DV.

DV: words recalled

Write it as
The number of words recalled out of 20.

Exam tipNot just "memory": say exactly what was counted.

  1. IV: age group

  3. DV: reaction time

IV: age group

Why
A group difference being compared is the IV.

Control: same task

Why
Everyone does the same task, so it is a control.

DV: reaction time

Write it as
Reaction time to the light in milliseconds.

4

The method

  1. Find the difference

    What differs between the conditions or groups is the IV.

  2. Find the measure

    What was counted, timed or scored at the end is the DV.

  3. Spot the controls

    Anything the same for everyone is a control, not the IV or DV.

  4. Use the study

    Write the variable with the study's details, not a definition.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Swapping them

    The IV is changed; the DV is measured. Check which is which.

  • ✗
    Picking a control

    Time allowed or the room is a control, not a variable.

  • ✗
    Vague DV

    "Memory" is not enough; say what was measured.

Worked explanation

  1. The two conditions differ in what the man wore: that is the IV (option A).
  2. Time allowed is the same for everyone, so it is a control (option B).
  3. What Thomas recorded was how many people stayed silent, so that is the DV.
