Retrograde and anterograde amnesia

Written

Retrograde amnesia loses memories from before the damage. Anterograde amnesia stops new long-term memories forming after it.

The method

  1. Past or new?

    Is the test about before onset, or new learning?

  2. Define the type

    Say what is lost in that type.

  3. Predict

    Use the details of the test in the question.

  4. Data

    Say which group struggled on which task and quote scores.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Swapped types

    Retrograde: past lost. Anterograde: new lost.

  • ✗
    Everything forgotten

    Each type loses only some memories.

  • ✗
    No numbers

    Data conclusions need figures.

Worked explanation

  1. The faces were famous during the five years before the test, which includes time before the onset six months ago.
  2. Retrograde amnesia means loss of memories from before onset.
  3. Predict difficulty recognising faces from that earlier period.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

In retrograde amnesia a person loses memories of information and events from the time before their amnesia began. As the famous people were in the news during the five years before the test, if the patient has retrograde amnesia Ivana is likely to find that they fail to recognise many of the 20 faces, especially those known before the amnesia started six months ago.

How the marks are earned

One mark for defining retrograde amnesia as loss of pre-onset memories; one mark for predicting poor recognition of the famous faces.
