Sleep disorders: insomnia and narcolepsy

Written

Insomnia: you cannot get to sleep or stay asleep. Narcolepsy: you cannot stay awake, with sudden sleep attacks in the day.

4

The method

  1. Choose the disorder

    Awake at night: insomnia. Sudden day sleep: narcolepsy.

  2. Define it

    Describe the disorder by its symptoms.

  3. Link the result

    Show how the symptoms cause the person's problem.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Mixing them up

    Insomnia: cannot sleep. Narcolepsy: cannot stay awake.

  • ✗
    Just tired

    Narcolepsy needs sudden sleep attacks.

  • ✗
    Copying the scenario

    Explain the symptoms; do not just repeat them.

Sleep disorders: insomnia and narcolepsy · June 2025 · Paper 2 · Question 30 · part (whole) · 2 marks

30 Dennis always feels tired. He visits his doctor to discuss his symptoms. Dennis tells the doctor that he goes to bed early and falls asleep quickly. However, he wakes up an hour later and cannot get back to sleep. He struggles to concentrate at work as he is so tired and has started to make a number of errors. His doctor suggests that he may have a sleep disorder. Explain, using one sleep disorder, why Dennis is constantly tired and making errors at work.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Symptoms: wakes after an hour and can't get back to sleep → insomnia.
  2. Define insomnia.
  3. Link lost sleep to tiredness and errors.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Insomnia is a disorder of sleep in which a person has trouble getting to sleep or, once asleep, wakes up and cannot drop off again, so they do not get enough sleep and feel unrefreshed and drowsy the next day. Dennis falls asleep but wakes an hour later and stays awake, so he loses most of his night's sleep; this leaves him constantly tired, so he cannot concentrate at work and makes errors.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1 for an accurate account of insomnia; mark 2 for linking Dennis's waking and lost sleep to tiredness and errors.
