Types of non-verbal communication

Written

Non-verbal communication sends messages without words. It includes facial expressions, eye contact, and body language such as posture and gestures.

Find the four types of non-verbal communication and what each shows.

  1. Facial expression
  2. Eye contact
  3. Posture

Facial expression

Shows
Friendliness or interest.

Eye contact

Shows
Looking away can signal discomfort.

Posture

Shows
Closed posture can signal nerves.
Write it as
How the body is held, e.g. crossed arms.

Gesture

Shows
A hand movement that carries a meaning.

Exam tipPosture is how the body is held; a gesture is a movement. Do not mix them up.

The method

  1. Name the type

    Facial expression, eye contact, posture or gesture.

  2. Define it simply

    One short sentence.

  3. Example and emotion

    Quote the behaviour and say what feeling it shows.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Posture vs gesture

    Held position vs a movement.

  • ✗
    Verbal examples

    Shouting or tone of words is not NVC.

  • ✗
    No emotion

    Say what the signal shows.

Types of non-verbal communication · June 2025 · Paper 2 · Question 37 · part (whole) · 2 marks

37 Jamie is sitting in his parents’ garden. He can see through the kitchen window that his mother is on the telephone. After she finishes her telephone call, she places her hands on her head. She then points at his father and crosses her arms in front of her body. Jamie sees this and wonders why his mother is annoyed. Explain, using body language, why Jamie believed that his mother was annoyed after her telephone call.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Define body language.
  2. Pick out the mother's actions: hands on head, pointing, arms crossed.
  3. Say these signal frustration or annoyance.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Body language means using movements and the way we hold our body (gestures and posture) to show our feelings without words. Jamie's mother held her head in her hands, pointed at his father and folded her arms across her body; these are closed, negative gestures and posture which usually signal frustration, so Jamie read her as annoyed.

How the marks are earned

One mark for explaining body language, one for applying it to the mother's specific gestures and posture.
