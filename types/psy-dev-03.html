Knowledge card

Mindsets and praise (Dweck; Gunderson et al., 2013)

Written

A fixed mindset says ability cannot change; a growth mindset says effort makes you better. Praising effort builds a growth mindset; praising being clever builds a fixed one.

1

Fixed or growth?

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

3 clues to find

Find which praise builds a growth mindset and which builds a fixed one.

  1. Person praise: fixed
  2. Process praise: growth
  3. Strategy praise: growth

Person praise: fixed

Why
It praises ability, as if it cannot change.

Process praise: growth

Why
It praises effort, which can grow.

Strategy praise: growth

Why
It praises the method used.

The method

  1. Fixed or growth?

    Decide which mindset the praise or behaviour matches.

  2. State the idea

    Say what that mindset means or which praise builds it.

  3. Apply it

    Quote what the person did, or give exact words of praise.

  4. Gunderson details

    Know the ages, the praise types and the finding.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Swapping mindsets

    Fixed means ability is set; growth means it can improve.

  • ✗
    Clever praise

    Praising cleverness builds a fixed mindset, not growth.

  • ✗
    Example only

    Explain the mindset idea as well as giving the praise.

Mindsets and praise (Dweck; Gunderson et al., 2013) · June 2023 · Paper 1 · Question 2 · part (whole) · 2 marks

2 Emily is trying to solve a maths puzzle given by her teacher. She is struggling to get the solution. Martha is a teaching assistant who is sitting with Emily and wants to encourage Emily to develop a growth mindset. Emily is trying to solve the puzzle again. Explain one way that Martha could praise Emily to try to encourage a growth mindset. You should refer to growth mindset in your answer.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Growth mindset comes from praising effort and strategies, not talent.
  2. Choose wording Martha could use that praises Emily's effort on the puzzle.
  3. Say how this helps Emily keep going.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

A growth mindset develops when a child is praised for the effort and strategies they use rather than for being naturally clever. So Martha could tell Emily, 'I can see how hard you are working on this puzzle, keep trying different ways', which praises her effort and helps her believe she can improve if she keeps going.

How the marks are earned

One mark for linking a growth mindset to praise for effort; one mark for a suitable example of effort praise for Emily during the puzzle.
