Willingham's learning theory

Written

Willingham says factual knowledge comes before skill, and skills grow with practice and effort. Turn the idea into a clear activity for the skill in the question.

4 strategies to find

Find the Willingham idea behind each activity.

  1. Cognitive: right challenge
  2. Physical: practise
  3. Social: see it, do it
  4. Facts before skill

Cognitive: right challenge

Idea
Practice with tasks not too easy or too hard.

Physical: practise

Idea
Repeat with feedback until it is automatic.

Social: see it, do it

Idea
Watch good behaviour, then practise it.

Facts before skill

Idea
You need facts before you can think critically.

Exam tipNever write 'skill comes before knowledge': it is the other way round.

The method

  1. Which development?

    Cognitive, physical or social: each has its own strategy.

  2. State the idea

    Practice and effort, facts first, modelling, or the right challenge.

  3. Make it concrete

    Describe an activity using the task in the question.

  4. Data questions

    Link the trend to practice and quote the figures.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Reversed idea

    Facts come before skill, not after.

  • ✗
    Vague tips

    'Study harder' names no theory idea.

  • ✗
    Wrong theorist

    Assimilation and equilibrium belong to Piaget.

Worked explanation

  1. The class is learning a new cognitive task: the order of the planets.
  2. Willingham: skills improve with repeated, effortful practice.
  3. Suggest a specific repeated activity Mr Harris could set.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Willingham argues that a new skill only improves when learners practise it again and again and put effort into it. Mr Harris could give his class a short daily activity where they arrange planet cards in order from the Sun and check their answers, repeating it until they can do it quickly and without mistakes.

How the marks are earned

One mark for the idea that effortful practice improves a skill; one mark for a specific practice activity for the planets task.
