Finding the median

Calculation

The median is the middle value once the data are put in order. With two middle values, take halfway between them.

1

Find the middle

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

3 values to find

Find the first step, the repeated value and the median.

  1. Lowest first
  2. Keep both 6s
  3. Median = 6

Lowest first

Why
Ordering smallest to largest often earns its own mark.

Keep both 6s

Why
Drop a repeat and the middle shifts.

Median = 6

Why
7 values: (7 + 1) ÷ 2 = 4th position.

2 values to find

Six values: find the two middle values.

  1. Middle value one
  2. Middle value two

Middle value one

Why
(6 + 1) ÷ 2 = 3.5, so between 3rd and 4th.

Middle value two

(7 + 8) ÷ 2 = 7.5

2

The calculation

Median position = (n + 1) ÷ 2, in the ordered list

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

  • Order the values first, smallest to largest.
  • Two middle values: add them and halve.

Try itFind the median of 2, 9, 4, 1, 7.

  1. In order: 1, 2, 4, 7, 9
  2. n = 5, so (5 + 1) ÷ 2 = 3rd value
  3. Median = 4

5

The method

  1. Put in order

    Smallest to largest, keeping repeats.

  2. Find the position

    Work out (n + 1) ÷ 2.

  3. Read the middle

    Odd n: one value. Even n: two values, add and halve.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Not ordered

    The middle of a jumbled list is not the median.

  • ✗
    Missed repeat

    Count every value, even the same one twice.

  • ✗
    One of two

    With an even count, average the two middle values.
