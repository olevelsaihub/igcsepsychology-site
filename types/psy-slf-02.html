The method

  1. Classify

    Is the regard conditional or unconditional?

  2. Define it

    One exact sentence.

  3. Give the effect

    Say what it does to self-esteem or behaviour.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    All praise is unconditional

    Praise tied to success is conditional.

  • ✗
    No effect

    Always add the effect on self-esteem.

  • ✗
    Rogers vs Maslow

    Positive regard is Rogers, not Maslow.

Worked explanation

  1. Define conditional positive regard.
  2. Explain the consequence: constant pressure to earn approval, so self-worth becomes fragile and self-esteem drops.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Conditional positive regard means a person only receives love or approval when they do what others expect, for example getting top grades. Because they feel valued only when they succeed, they may keep doubting whether they are good enough, which lowers their self-esteem.

How the marks are earned

One mark for defining conditional positive regard; one for its effect on self-esteem.
