The method

  1. Find the gap

    Which level is unmet?

  2. State the rule

    Lower needs come before higher ones.

  3. Link to behaviour

    Show how the unmet need changes what they do.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong order

    Physiological, safety, love, esteem, self-actualisation.

  • ✗
    Need with no rule

    Add that lower needs come first.

  • ✗
    Top as low

    Self-actualisation is the top, growth need.

Maslow's hierarchy of needs · June 2025 · Paper 2 · Question 16 · part (whole) · 2 marks

16 Raymond’s manager noticed that his performance at work had declined in recent weeks. She overheard Raymond say to his colleagues that his house was really cold as his heating had broken and he could not afford to get it fixed. This meant that he was finding it difficult to sleep. Explain, using Maslow’s (1943) hierarchy of needs, why Raymond’s performance at work may have declined.

Worked explanation

  1. Raymond is cold and cannot sleep: physiological needs unmet.
  2. Maslow: these basic survival needs must be met first.
  3. So he cannot focus on higher needs like doing well at work (esteem).
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Maslow (1943) said that physiological needs such as warmth and sleep are the base of the hierarchy and must be satisfied before a person can focus on anything higher. Raymond's broken heating leaves him cold and unable to sleep, so his basic needs are unmet and he lacks the energy to concentrate, which is why his work has got worse.

How the marks are earned

One mark for knowing that physiological needs form the base and come first; one for applying this to Raymond's cold home and lost sleep.
