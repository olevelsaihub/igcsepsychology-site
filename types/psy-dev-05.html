How the brain develops before birth

Written

About three weeks after conception the brain starts as forebrain, midbrain and hindbrain. The hindbrain gives the cerebellum and the medulla.

The method

  1. Name the region

    Say which swelling it comes from.

  2. Give its job

    State its main function.

  3. Add a second fact

    Timing, an example, or what damage would do.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Cerebellum vs cerebrum

    Cerebellum: coordination. Cerebrum: thinking.

  • ✗
    Medulla is automatic

    It runs breathing and heart rate, not thinking.

  • ✗
    One fact only

    A 2-mark describe needs two points.

Worked explanation

  1. Fact 1: when the medulla develops.
  2. Fact 2: what it controls.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

The medulla develops from the hindbrain and starts to form at around six to seven weeks of pregnancy. It controls automatic, involuntary functions that keep us alive, such as breathing and heart rate.

How the marks are earned

One mark for the timing or origin; a second mark for its role in automatic functions such as breathing.
