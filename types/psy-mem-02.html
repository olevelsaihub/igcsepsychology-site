Knowledge card

How long and how much: duration and capacity

Written

Duration is how long a store holds information; capacity is how much it holds. STM: about 15-30 seconds and 7 ± 2 items. LTM: a lifetime, unlimited.

The method

  1. Time or amount?

    Duration is time; capacity is amount.

  2. Give the figure

    Use the right number for the store asked.

  3. Predict

    Apply the figure to the scenario, e.g. failure beyond 9 items.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Swapped meanings

    Capacity is not a time.

  • ✗
    All 10 digits

    Most people fail beyond about 9.

  • ✗
    Small LTM

    LTM capacity is thought to be unlimited.

Worked explanation

  1. STM holds about 7 plus or minus 2 items (Miller, 1956).
  2. Nishka's lists grow from 4 to 10 numbers.
  3. Predict: nearly all recall the short lists; few manage the longest.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Miller (1956) found that short-term memory can hold about seven items, plus or minus two, so most people can keep between five and nine items. Nishka is likely to find that almost all participants can recall the four- and five-number lists, but the number of successful participants falls as the lists get longer, with hardly anyone recalling the ten-number list correctly.

How the marks are earned

One mark for the STM capacity of around 7 plus or minus 2; one mark for predicting success on short lists and failure near ten numbers.
