Next digit 7: round up

Why
2 d.p. keeps 6.43; the next digit is 7, so 6.44.

Start at first non-zero

Why
Leading zeros do not count: 5 and 8, then 6 rounds up.

First number 1 to under 10

Why
3.82 is between 1 and 10; the decimal moved 6 places.

Round first, then add

Why
An estimate uses rounded numbers, not the exact 799.

Standard form

a × 10ⁿ, where 1 ≤ a < 10

  • 39.98 × 10⁶ is not standard form: a must be under 10.
  • Round only at the end.

5

The method

  1. Spot the form

    Standard form, estimate, d.p. or s.f.?

  2. Calculate

    Exact numbers, or rounded first for an estimate.

  3. Round at the end

    Look at the next digit: 5 or more rounds up.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    a too big

    In standard form the first number is under 10.

  • ✗
    d.p. or s.f.?

    Decimal places count after the point; s.f. from the first non-zero digit.

  • ✗
    Exact when estimating

    An estimate must show rounded numbers.
