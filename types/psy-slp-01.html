Sleep stages, REM and why sleep matters

Written

Sleep cycles about every 90 minutes through four NREM stages and REM. Good sleep restores the body and consolidates memory.

The method

  1. State it

    Give the stage feature or benefit accurately.

  2. Link it

    Show how it helps the person or explains what was seen.

  3. Check details

    REM: active brain, still body. Deep sleep: delta waves.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Moving in REM

    Muscles are paralysed in REM.

  • ✗
    Deep sleep vs REM

    Stages 3-4 are deep; REM is when vivid dreams happen.

  • ✗
    No link

    A benefit with no link to the task loses a mark.

Aditi will also need to take a test to show she knows about medication, patient care and her legal responsibilities. Explain one benefit of sleep that may help Aditi to do well during her interview.

Worked explanation

  1. Pick a benefit: memory consolidation (or concentration).
  2. Link to Aditi's test on medication and her decision tasks.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Getting enough sleep helps the brain consolidate memories, so things learnt during the day are stored more securely and are easier to recall. This would help Aditi remember what she has revised about medication, patient care and her legal duties when she sits the test, and a well-rested brain would also keep her alert for the decision-making activities.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1 for an accurate benefit of sleep; mark 2 for showing how it would help Aditi in her interview tasks.
