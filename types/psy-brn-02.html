Knowledge card

Left and right hemispheres, corpus callosum and sex differences

Written

The hemispheres are lateralised: the left handles language and the right side of the body; the right handles space, faces and the left side. The corpus callosum links them.

4

The method

  1. Find the structure

    Which hemisphere, or the corpus callosum?

  2. State its function

    Language left, spatial right, the corpus callosum connects.

  3. Predict the effect

    Apply it to the person, remembering the crossover.

  4. Sex differences

    Use research and say results are mixed.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Swapped sides

    Left is language; right is spatial.

  • ✗
    No crossover

    A left-side stroke weakens the right side.

  • ✗
    Sex differences as fact

    Rilea et al. (2005) found differences on some tasks only.

Worked explanation

  1. Recall research: Rilea et al. (2005) reported sex differences in lateralisation and some spatial tasks.
  2. Predict who will do better on the map task.
  3. Link to finding the cake shop more quickly.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Rilea et al. (2005) reported differences in lateralisation between males and females and found that men did better on some spatial tasks. As map reading is a spatial task, the male is likely to plan a faster route and reach the cake shop before the female.

How the marks are earned

One mark for accurate research evidence on sex differences; one for applying it to the map task.
