Knowledge card

Types of data

Written

Quantitative data is numbers; qualitative data is words or descriptions. Primary data is collected first-hand for the study; secondary data already existed.

4

The method

  1. Numbers or words?

    Numbers are quantitative; words are qualitative.

  2. Who collected it?

    First-hand for this study is primary; already existing is secondary.

  3. Add an example

    When asked, give a specific item from the study.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Ratings

    Scale ratings are numbers, so quantitative.

  • ✗
    Mixing the pairs

    Primary is not the same as quantitative.

  • ✗
    Own questionnaire

    A researcher's own questionnaire is primary data.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Define qualitative data as data in words rather than numbers.
  2. Give an example fitting Davide's further research.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Qualitative data is descriptive information in words, images or meanings rather than numbers. For example, Davide could ask pupils the open question 'How do you feel about the school's ban on unhealthy food?' and record what they say.

How the marks are earned

One mark for the definition, one for a suitable example.
