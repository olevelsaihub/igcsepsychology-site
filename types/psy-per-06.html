Perceptual set: why we see what we expect

Written

Perceptual set is a readiness to see things in a certain way. Motivation, expectation, emotion and culture all bias what we perceive.

The method

  1. Find the factor

    Motivation, expectation, emotion or culture?

  2. Say what they saw

    Name what the person perceived, in context.

  3. Justify

    Explain how the factor sets the mind; name research if you can.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong factor

    Hunger is motivation, not emotion.

    No concept

Perceptual set: why we see what we expect · June 2025 · Paper 2 · Question 24 · part (whole) · 2 marks

24 A group of students were recruited for an experiment. They were instructed not to eat prior to arrival at a laboratory at 9:00 am. After arrival they were split into two groups: Group 1 remained in the laboratory before testing at 9:30 am. • • Group 2 were told to eat breakfast before testing at 10:00 am. During the test, each group were briefly shown 20 images and asked to identify what they saw. Group 1 identified more food-related images than Group 2. No images of food were actually shown. Explain one reason why Group 1 identified more food-related images than Group 2. You must use influences on perceptual set to justify your answer.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Notice Group 1 had not eaten; Group 2 had.
  2. Identify motivation (hunger) as the influence.
  3. Justify with the concept, citing research on hunger and perception.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Group 1 had not eaten, so they were hungry and motivated to look for food, which made food-like shapes in the images more noticeable to them than to Group 2, who had just had breakfast. Motivation is an influence on perceptual set: a bodily need such as hunger sets us to perceive stimuli connected to that need, as Gilchrist and Nesberg (1952) showed when hungry participants saw food pictures as brighter.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1 for identifying hunger/motivation as the reason Group 1 saw food; mark 2 for justifying through motivation as an influence on perceptual set.
