Calculating percentages

Calculation

A percentage is the part divided by the whole, times 100. Most lost marks come from dividing by the wrong whole.

1

Find the part and the whole

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

3 numbers to find

You need the % of all participants who obeyed. Find the part, the whole and the wrong total.

  1. The part: 27 obeyed
  2. The whole: 48
  3. Not this total

The part: 27 obeyed

Why
Obeyed in both conditions: 18 + 9.

The whole: 48

Why
"All participants" means everyone in both conditions.
Write it as
27 ÷ 48 × 100 = 56.25%

Not this total

Why
24 is only the uniform condition, not all participants.

Exam tipUnderline the words after "percentage of" to find the whole.

2 numbers to find

Find the change and the original value.

  1. Original: 20
  2. Change: 5

Original: 20

Why
Percentage change always divides by the starting value.

Change: 5

Write it as
5 ÷ 20 × 100 = 25% increase

Exam tipDividing by 25 gives 20%, which is wrong.

0 of 2 found
2

The calculation

Percentage = part ÷ whole × 100

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

  • Percentage change: divide the change by the original value.
  • Round only at the end, and add the % sign.

Try it14 of 40 participants conformed. What percentage conformed?

  1. Part = 14, whole = 40
  2. 14 ÷ 40 = 0.35
  3. 0.35 × 100 = 35%

5

The method

  1. Find the part

    Add cells if the group is spread across the table.

  2. Find the whole

    Check exactly which total the question names.

  3. Divide and × 100

    Part ÷ whole, then multiply by 100.

  4. Round and add %

    Round as told and write the % sign.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong total

    One condition is not "all participants".

  • ✗
    Upside down

    The whole always goes on the bottom.

  • ✗
    Rounding early

    Keep every digit until the final answer.

Worked explanation

  1. Total participants: 22 + 8 + 11 + 19 = 60.
  2. Obeyed: 22 + 11 = 33.
  3. 33 / 60 x 100 = 55%.
