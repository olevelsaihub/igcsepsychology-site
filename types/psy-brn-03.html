4

The method

  1. Which hemisphere?

    Left hand or left field means right hemisphere.

  2. Recall the finding

    The right cannot name it but can recognise it.

  3. Predict

    Say what the person can and cannot do.

  4. Evaluate

    Small sample, epilepsy and culture, linked to the person.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong side

    Left hand goes to the right hemisphere.

  • ✗
    "Cannot feel"

    They can feel and pick it; they cannot name it.

  • ✗
    Sample size

    It was 11 patients.

Worked explanation

  1. Left hand: information goes to the right hemisphere.
  2. Right hemisphere cannot produce speech; corpus callosum cut so no transfer.
  3. Predict Hina cannot say 'marble'.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Sperry (1968) found that split-brain patients who felt an object with their left hand could not name it, because the information went to the right hemisphere, which cannot speak and could not pass it to the left. Hina picked the marble with her left hand, so she probably cannot tell the audience it was a marble, although she could point to it or pick it out again.

How the marks are earned

One mark for Sperry's finding about naming objects felt with the left hand; one for applying it to Hina.
