Knowledge card

Calculating ratios

Calculation

A ratio compares two amounts as a:b, in the order the question gives, then simplified as far as it will go.

1

Which two numbers, and in which order?

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

3 numbers to find

You need the ratio of people who conformed to all people. Find both numbers and the trap.

  1. First number: 16
  2. Second number: 40
  3. Only for "the rest"

First number: 16

Why
The group named first in the question goes first.

Second number: 40

Why
"All" means the whole group.
Write it as
16:40 = 2:5

Only for "the rest"

Why
Use 24 only if asked for conformed to did not conform.
Write it as
16:24 = 2:3
0 of 3 found
2

The calculation

a : b, then divide both sides by the HCF

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

  • Keep the order of the question: 2:5 is not 5:2.
  • Write a ratio with a colon, not as a fraction.

Try it18 of 45 participants disobeyed. Give the ratio of those who disobeyed to all participants.

  1. Write 18:45
  2. HCF of 18 and 45 is 9
  3. 18 ÷ 9 = 2, 45 ÷ 9 = 5, so 2:5

Does it score?

Exam question

16 out of 40 participants conformed. Calculate the ratio of those who conformed to all participants, in its simplest form. (2 marks)

Decide first: would this answer earn the mark? Then tap it to check.

Tap an answer to see if it earns the mark.

5

The method

  1. Read the order

    Note which group comes first and which second.

  2. Find both numbers

    Add cells if needed; "all" means the total.

  3. Write a:b

    Show the unsimplified ratio first.

  4. Simplify fully

    Divide both sides by the HCF.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong order

    Swap the order and the answer is wrong.

  • ✗
    Half simplified

    8:20 still divides by 4: keep going to 2:5.

  • ✗
    All or the rest?

    Check which the question asks for.

Worked explanation

  1. Disobeyed: 8 + 19 = 27.
  2. All participants: 60.
  3. 27:60, HCF 3, gives 9:20.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Disobeyed = 8 + 19 = 27 All = 60 27:60 = 9:20

How the marks are earned

One mark for 27, one for 9:20.

Calculation check

  • 8+19 = 27
  • 27 ÷ 3 = 9
  • 60 ÷ 3 = 20
