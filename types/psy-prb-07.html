Knowledge card

Drug treatments for depression and addiction

Written

Drug treatments change brain or body chemistry: SSRIs keep more serotonin in the synapse, and methadone or disulfiram help people stop an addiction.

4

The method

  1. Which problem?

    Depression or addiction: pick a fitting drug.

  2. How it works

    State its effect on the brain or body.

  3. Link to the person

    Show how it changes their symptoms or situation.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    "Adds serotonin"

    SSRIs stop reuptake, keeping serotonin in the synapse.

  • ✗
    Wrong drug

    Do not give an SSRI for an addiction question.

  • ✗
    Two-in-one

    Side effects and nausea are one point, not two.

Worked explanation

  1. Weakness 1: relapse if the medication is stopped.
  2. Justify with Claire needing motivation to keep taking it.
  3. Weakness 2: substitute drugs can be addictive.
  4. Justify with the risk of swapping one dependence for another.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

1 Claire might stop taking the medication, and then her cravings would return and she could start using again. So the treatment only works if she is determined enough to keep taking it every day. 2 Some drugs given for addiction, such as methadone, can be addictive themselves. Claire could become dependent on the treatment drug instead, which brings new health risks and means her addiction has not really been solved.

How the marks are earned

One mark for each weakness and one for each justification linked to Claire.
