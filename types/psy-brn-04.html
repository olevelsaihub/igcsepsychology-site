Neurons, synapses and the central nervous system

Written

A neuron carries an electrical impulse; at the synapse, neurotransmitters cross the gap and bind to receptors. The CNS is the brain and spinal cord.

The method

  1. Use the terms

    Impulse, vesicle, neurotransmitter, synaptic gap, receptor.

  2. Keep the order

    Release, cross, bind, then reuptake or breakdown.

  3. CNS scenarios

    Messages travel via the spinal cord; link to the symptom.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong direction

    Neurotransmitters cross the gap, not along the neuron.

  • ✗
    CNS mix-up

    The CNS is only the brain and spinal cord.

    Name only

    Say what each part does.

Neurons, synapses and the central nervous system · June 2024 · Paper 1 · Question 20 · part (whole) · 2 marks

20 Serinda was recently involved in a motorbike accident. A doctor is checking if her central nervous system is functioning as expected. Before the accident, Serinda had no damage to her central nervous system. The doctor places hot and cold pads on Serinda’s arms and legs. Serinda says she cannot feel the hot and cold pads on her legs. The doctor concludes Serinda has damaged her central nervous system. Explain one reason why the doctor concluded this. You should refer to the role of the central nervous system in your answer.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. State that the spinal cord carries messages between body and brain.
  2. Link the lack of feeling in the legs to messages not reaching the brain.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

The central nervous system is made up of the brain and spinal cord, and the spinal cord carries sensory messages from the body up to the brain. Serinda cannot feel the hot and cold pads on her legs, so the signals from her legs are not getting through the spinal cord to her brain, which suggests her CNS has been damaged.

How the marks are earned

One mark for the role of the CNS; one for applying it to Serinda not feeling the pads.
