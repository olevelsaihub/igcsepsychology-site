The method

  1. Recall the detail

    Aim, method, damaged area or behaviour change.

  2. Compare the damage

    Ventromedial: same as Gage. Broca's area: not in Gage.

  3. Predict or evaluate

    Same area means similar problems; extra areas cannot be predicted.

Do not lose marks here

    "His brain"

    They used his skull and a computer model.

  • ✗
    Broca's area

    It was not damaged in Gage.

    Story only

    Link Gage to the person in the question.

Worked explanation

  1. Strength: same ventromedial damage as Gage.
  2. Justify: so similar decision-making problems are likely.
  3. Weakness: Broca's area damaged in Marvin but not in Gage.
  4. Justify: so Damasio cannot predict his language problems.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Strength: Marvin's ventromedial region is damaged, just like Phineas Gage's, who became impulsive after his injury. Because Damasio et al. linked this area to difficulty making rational decisions, it is reasonable to predict Marvin will have similar problems, which makes the study useful here. Weakness: Marvin also has an injury to Broca's area, an area that was intact in Gage. This could affect his speech and his language teaching, so Damasio et al. cannot fully predict how Marvin will be affected.

How the marks are earned

One mark for each strength/weakness identified and one for each justification linked to Marvin.
