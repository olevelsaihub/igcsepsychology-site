Knowledge card

Ethics in research

Written

Treat participants fairly: get informed consent, avoid deception or debrief, keep data confidential, allow the right to withdraw and protect them from harm.

4

The method

  1. Name the issue

    Pick a recognised issue: consent, deception, confidentiality, withdrawal or harm.

  2. Say the action

    Describe what the researcher would do in this study.

  3. Make them differ

    For "two ways", use two different issues.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Issue with no action

    Naming "consent" alone does not say what to do.

  • ✗
    Same point twice

    Numbers and pseudonyms are both confidentiality.

  • ✗
    Forgetting parents

    Under 16s need consent from a parent.

Worked explanation

  1. Children are six, so consent must come from parents.
  2. Pick a second, different issue: confidentiality or right to withdraw.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

1. Gary could ask the children's parents for informed consent, explaining what the button task involves before the children take part. 2. Gary could keep the children's identities confidential by giving each child a number rather than using their names in his results.

How the marks are earned

One mark for each different, workable ethical step for this study.
