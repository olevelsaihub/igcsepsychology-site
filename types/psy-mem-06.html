Knowledge card

Peterson and Peterson (1959): how long STM lasts

Written

Peterson and Peterson (1959) showed STM fades within seconds when rehearsal is blocked. Saying items aloud helped recall more than silent repetition.

4

The method

  1. Recall the procedure

    Trigram, number, count back, light, recall.

  2. State the finding

    Fast forgetting without rehearsal; aloud helps.

  3. Apply it

    Link the finding to how the person remembered.

  4. Evaluate

    Compare artificial trigrams with real-life memory.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Words, not trigrams

    They recalled trigrams of three consonants.

  • ✗
    Wrong intervals

    Multiples of 3, up to 18 seconds.

  • ✗
    Finding missing

    Say what the study found before applying it.

Worked explanation

  1. Saturday: silent repetition; Sunday: repeating aloud; Sunday recall was better.
  2. Peterson and Peterson found vocal rehearsal led to better recall than silent rehearsal.
  3. Link the finding to Chelsea's two shopping trips.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

In Peterson and Peterson's (1959) research, participants who rehearsed out loud they recalled more trigrams than when they rehearsed silently. On Sunday Chelsea said the items out loud as she walked, but on Saturday she only repeated them in her head, which explains why she remembered more of the shopping list on Sunday.

How the marks are earned

One mark for the finding that vocal rehearsal improves recall; one mark for linking it to Chelsea's spoken repetition on Sunday.
