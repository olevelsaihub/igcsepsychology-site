Hormones and sleep: the pineal gland and melatonin

Written

In darkness the pineal gland releases more melatonin, a hormone that makes us sleepy. Light reduces melatonin, so we feel alert.

The method

  1. Give the chain

    Light level, then pineal gland, then melatonin up or down.

  2. Say the effect

    More melatonin: sleepy. Less: alert.

  3. Link and justify

    Apply to the person; give a reason for any strength or weakness.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong way round

    Dark raises melatonin; light lowers it.

  • ✗
    Gland vs hormone

    The pineal gland releases melatonin.

  • ✗
    No link

    Tie the change to the person's sleep problem.

Worked explanation

  1. Point 1: pineal gland responds to light information.
  2. Point 2: in darkness it releases melatonin, which causes sleepiness.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

The pineal gland receives signals about how much light the eyes are detecting, passed on from the body clock in the brain. When it gets dark, the pineal gland releases more of the hormone melatonin, which makes the person feel drowsy and ready for sleep; in bright light it releases less, so they feel more awake.

How the marks are earned

Mark 1 for the pineal gland responding to light information; mark 2 for melatonin being released and causing sleepiness.
