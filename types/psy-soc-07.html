Knowledge card

Culture and society

Written

A society is a large group sharing an area and its rules and norms. A culture is the shared beliefs, values and customs passed down the generations.

The method

  1. Define it

    One sentence with the key idea.

  2. Society

    Shared area, laws and norms.

  3. Culture

    Shared beliefs, values, customs.

  4. Describe an effect

    Contrast individualist and collectivist.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Same thing

    Society and culture are different.

  • ✗
    Example only

    Define first.

  • ✗
    No psychology

    Link culture to behaviour, e.g. conformity.

Worked explanation

  1. Recall the key idea: people living together who share rules and norms.
  2. Write it in one sentence.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

A society is a large group of people living in the same place who follow a shared set of rules, laws and expected ways of behaving.
