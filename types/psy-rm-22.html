Standardise

Change
Read the same script to everyone.
Fixes
Differences in instructions cannot affect times.

Repeated measures

Change
Test the same people with and without caffeine.
Fixes
Removes differences between participants.

Independent rater

Change
Someone who does not know the hypothesis scores.
Fixes
Reduces bias in the scoring.

More trials

Change
Ten trials each and take the mean.
Fixes
One slow trial will not distort the result.

4

The method

  1. Find the flaw

    In the task, instructions, design, measure or ethics.

  2. Suggest a change

    Say exactly what Ali should do differently.

  3. Explain the fix

    Say what the change stops and why that matters.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    No reason

    A change alone gets at most one mark.

  • ✗
    Generic reason

    "More reliable" needs a because.

  • ✗
    Already done

    Do not suggest something the study already had.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Flaw: unusual objects like a boat's anchor may be unfamiliar to many people.
  2. Change: use everyday objects such as a cup, a pen or a toothbrush.
  3. Why: failure to name them would then show the brain injury, not lack of familiarity.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Matthew could have used pictures of very common everyday objects, such as a cup or a spoon, instead of things like a boat's anchor. This is because the patient might not be able to name an anchor simply because they have rarely seen one, so using familiar objects would make it clearer that any failure to name them is due to the brain injury.

How the marks are earned

One mark for the improvement and one for the linked justification.
