Symptoms of depression and addiction, and their impact

ICD symptoms and features of depression and addiction, and how mental health problems affect individuals and society.

Type 1 3 Psychological problems PSY-PRB-01

★☆☆☆☆ · 3 of 14 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Easy.

The question asks you to state or describe symptoms, features or effects of depression or addiction, often with the word ICD, or asks how a problem affects a person or society.

Knowledge card

Symptoms of depression and addiction, and their impact

Written

Depression is persistent low mood or loss of pleasure; addiction is losing control over use. Both affect the individual and society.

3 features to find

Find the three symptoms of depression.

  1. Persistent low mood
  2. Loss of interest
  3. Feeling worthless

Persistent low mood

Why it matters
Low mood most days for at least two weeks is the core sign.

Loss of interest

Write it as
He has lost interest or pleasure in activities.

Feeling worthless

Write it as
He has feelings of worthlessness.

Exam tipA family history is a possible cause, not a symptom.

3 features to find

Find the three features of addiction.

  1. Impaired control
  2. Tolerance
  3. Withdrawal

Impaired control

Write it as
He cannot control how much he drinks or stop.

Tolerance

Write it as
He needs more to get the same effect.

Withdrawal

Write it as
He has withdrawal symptoms when he stops.

4

The method

  1. Which problem?

    Decide: depression, addiction, or effects on a person or society.

  2. Use the proper term

    Write impaired control, withdrawal, persistent low mood.

  3. One point per mark

    Each mark needs a different symptom or feature.

  4. Develop if asked

    For describe or explain, add detail or link to the person.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Cause, not symptom

    Genes or stress are causes. List what the person shows.

  • ✗
    Mixing problems

    Depression symptoms score nothing on an addiction question.

  • ✗
    Wrong level

    Society means costs and lost work, not one person's feelings.

Worked explanation

  1. Spot that it is about addiction, not depression.
  2. Recall the ICD-11 features of dependence.
  3. Write two clearly different features, one per line.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

1 The person has impaired control over their use, e.g. they cannot limit how often or how much they take, or stop once they start. 2 The substance becomes a higher priority than other parts of life, such as health, work or family duties, and use continues even when it causes harm.

How the marks are earned

One mark for each distinct ICD feature of addiction.
