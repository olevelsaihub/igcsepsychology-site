Calculating the mean

Calculation

The mean is the total of all the values divided by how many values there are. It uses every score.

1

Find the total and the count

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

3 numbers to find

You need the mean before score. Find the total, the count and the trap.

  1. Total before: 33
  2. Six values
  3. Wrong column

Total before: 33

Why
Add every value in the column you were asked for.

Six values

Why
Count people, not the Total row.
Write it as
33 ÷ 6 = 5.5

Wrong column

Why
48 is the after total: 48 ÷ 6 = 8 is the after mean.
0 of 3 found
2

The calculation

Mean = total of all values ÷ number of values

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

  • Count repeated values every time they appear.
  • Round only to the places asked.

Try itScores: 4, 8, 6, 9, 5, 7. Calculate the mean.

  1. Total = 4 + 8 + 6 + 9 + 5 + 7 = 39
  2. There are 6 values
  3. 39 ÷ 6 = 6.5

5

The method

  1. Pick the right set

    Use only the column or group named.

  2. Add every value

    Include repeated values each time.

  3. Count the values

    This is what you divide by.

  4. Divide and round

    Round only as the question asks.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong count

    Do not count a Total row as a value.

  • ✗
    Mean or median?

    Mean adds and divides; median is the middle.

  • ✗
    Wrong column

    Before and after look alike: check the heading.
