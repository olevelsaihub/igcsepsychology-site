The method

  1. Recall the detail

    Sample, sessions measured, measures or findings.

  2. Compare the person

    Is their addiction like the internet addicts?

  3. Evaluate

    Generalising (addiction, culture) or method (self-report, no control).

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong timings

    Sessions 3, 8 and 12, then six months later.

  • ✗
    CBT in general

    Use Young's own sample and findings.

  • ✗
    Same weakness twice

    "Only USA" and "only Americans" are one point.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Weakness 1: different type of addiction (internet versus casino gambling).
  2. Justify: CBT effectiveness may differ for a face-to-face activity.
  3. Weakness 2: Young did not identify which addiction types benefit most.
  4. Justify: so the results may not represent Henrietta's case.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

1 Young's clients had an internet addiction, but Henrietta's addiction is gambling at a casino. CBT might work differently for an in-person activity, so Young's results may not show how well it will work for her. 2 Young did not say which kinds of internet use (such as gambling sites) improved most with CBT. This means we cannot tell whether CBT will help with Henrietta's gambling problem in particular, so the study may not represent her case.

How the marks are earned

One mark for each weakness identified and one for each linked justification.
