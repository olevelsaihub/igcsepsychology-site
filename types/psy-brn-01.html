The method

  1. Read the tasks

    Find what the person does badly and well.

  2. Match the ability

    Link it to the lobe or cerebellum.

  3. Explain

    Use the task details to show why that region.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Swapped lobes

    Parietal is touch; occipital is vision.

  • ✗
    Name only

    State the function as well as the region.

  • ✗
    Good results ignored

    They rule regions out.

Worked explanation

  1. Poor task: colour naming (vision). Good tasks: recall and pressure.
  2. Vision is processed in the occipital lobes.
  3. Link poor colour naming to occipital damage.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

The patient has probably damaged her occipital lobes. These lobes, at the back of the brain, process what the eyes see, including colour. She did well on recall and on feeling pressure but badly on naming the colour of the paper, which needs her visual system to interpret what she is looking at.

How the marks are earned

One mark for knowing that the occipital lobes handle vision; one for linking poor colour naming to that damage.
