Nature and nurture in depression and addiction

Written

Nature means inherited, biological influences such as genes; nurture means influences from the environment. Most problems involve both.

The method

  1. Which term?

    Nature, nurture, or the difference between them.

  2. Define it

    Inherited versus environmental.

  3. Use the scenario

    Parent with the same problem may be nature; friends or stress are nurture.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Copying a parent

    Copying is nurture, even though it involves a parent.

  • ✗
    One side only

    A difference question needs both terms.

  • ✗
    No scenario

    Use the person's details when there are some.

Nature and nurture in depression and addiction · June 2024 · Paper 1 · Question 13 · part (whole) · 2 marks

13 Dawood and his friends went shopping one weekend in the local shopping centre. He bought a pair of shoes and his friends said his shoes looked amazing. The next weekend, Dawood bought three more pairs of shoes from the shopping centre when he returned with his friends. His friends all commented again on how nice the shoes were. Over the next week, Dawood found he could not stop thinking about shoes, so he bought 15 more pairs from an online internet shop. Dawood’s older brother buys a lot of shoes too and frequently swaps shoes with Dawood. After buying 800 pairs of shoes over the next six months, Dawood is diagnosed with an addiction to shoes. Explain one way that nurture could be used to explain Dawood’s addiction to shoes.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Define nurture as environmental influence.
  2. Pick the friends' repeated praise as the environmental factor.
  3. Explain how it encouraged more buying.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Nurture refers to influences from the environment, such as other people and experiences, rather than inherited factors. Dawood's friends kept complimenting his new shoes each time he bought some, which rewarded his shoe buying and encouraged him to buy more and more, leading to his addiction.

How the marks are earned

One mark for understanding nurture; one for applying it to the friends' praise in the scenario.
