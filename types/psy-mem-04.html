The method

  1. Spot the process

    No attention, rehearsal, chunking or recall from LTM.

  2. State the model

    Say what that part of the model does.

  3. Apply

    What will the person remember or forget, and why?

  4. Evaluate

    Use HM, KF or the model being too simple, linked to the person.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Attention to LTM

    Attention only moves information into STM.

  • ✗
    Rehearsal in the sensory register

    Rehearsal happens in STM.

  • ✗
    Attention = rehearsal

    They are different processes.

The Multi-store Model of Memory · June 2024 · Paper 1 · Question 10 · part (whole) · 2 marks

10 Jack is trying to help his little brother remember the names of football players in their favourite football team. He reads out three names of players and asks his brother to say them back to him out loud. Jack does this until he has gone through the whole squad of football players. Over the next week, Jack repeats this process every day with his little brother. Explain what Jack is likely to find regarding his little brother’s memory of the football players. You should refer to the Multi‑store Model of Memory in your answer.

Written-answer check

Worked explanation

  1. The brother repeats names aloud every day for a week: that is rehearsal.
  2. The model says rehearsal moves information from STM to LTM.
  3. Predict that he remembers many of the names.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

The Multi-store Model of Memory says that information which is repeated over and over (rehearsal) is passed from the short-term store, which only holds it briefly, into the long-term store. Because Jack's brother says the players' names out loud every day for a week, Jack is likely to find that he can remember most of the squad's names, as they should now be held in his long-term memory.

How the marks are earned

One mark for stating that rehearsal transfers information from STM to LTM; one mark for predicting that the brother remembers many names because of daily repetition.
