Calculating fractions

Calculation

A fraction is the group over the total, then cut down to its lowest form. Read the condition exactly: "more than 20" leaves out 20.

1

Who is in the group?

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

3 scores to find

You need the fraction who scored more than 20. Find who counts and who does not.

  1. In the group
  2. 20 does not count
  3. Total: 8 students

In the group

Why
25 is more than 20. So are D, F and H: 4 students.

20 does not count

Why
"More than 20" starts above 20.

Exam tipCounting both 20s gives 6/8, the most common wrong answer.

Total: 8 students

Write it as
4/8 = 1/2
0 of 3 found
2

The calculation

Fraction = number in the group ÷ total

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

  • Divide top and bottom by the HCF until it will not go further.
  • Give a fraction, not a decimal or percentage.

Try it12 of 30 participants recalled more than 10 words. Give this as a fraction in its lowest form.

  1. Write 12/30
  2. HCF of 12 and 30 is 6
  3. 12 ÷ 6 = 2, 30 ÷ 6 = 5, so 2/5

Does it score?

Exam question

Using the table, calculate the fraction of students who scored more than 20. Give your answer in its lowest form. (2 marks)

Decide first: would this answer earn the mark? Then tap it to check.

Tap an answer to see if it earns the mark.

5

The method

  1. Read the condition

    More than, less than, or at least?

  2. Count the group

    Tick each value that meets it.

  3. Find the total

    Count everyone in the set.

  4. Simplify

    Divide top and bottom by the HCF.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Boundary value

    "More than 20" does not include 20.

  • ✗
    Not simplified

    4/8 loses a mark; write 1/2.

  • ✗
    Wrong form

    A decimal or % is not a fraction.
