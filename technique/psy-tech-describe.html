Psychology / Describe: a point and a linked development

Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Psychology · 1PS0

Describe: a point and a linked development

Describe how something works or what it involves: a process, a concept's effect, or how a method is done.

Command word: DescribeUsually 2 marksAbout 2 minutes for 2 marks

What Pearson asks

Describe how something works or what it involves: a process, a concept's effect, or how a method is done.

How the marks are earned

One mark for an accurate first point and a second for developing it, usually with an example, consequence or next step.

Method

  1. Make one clear, accurate point that answers the question.

    Why: Earns the first mark.

  2. Develop that same point with an example, an effect or the next step.

    Why: The second mark needs development, not a new unrelated point.

  3. Use the topic's key terms.

    Why: Precise vocabulary shows understanding.

Answer frame

[Concept] is/does ... (point), which means/for example ... (development).

Common errors

Inferred, not measured: Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

  • Writing two unrelated short points.
  • Being so vague that no psychology is shown.
  • Explaining why something is good or bad when only a description is asked.

Worked illustration

June 2023 · Paper 2 · Q36(whole)

The complete original question is shown; the illustration answers part (whole).

Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Eye contact is when people look into each other's eyes, and the way they look sends a message without words. For example, holding someone's gaze with widened pupils can show attraction, while looking away from someone can show sadness or embarrassment.

Why it earns the marks

First sentence explains what eye contact does as communication (point); second gives emotional examples (development).
