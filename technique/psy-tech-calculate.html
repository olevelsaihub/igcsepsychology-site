Calculate: show working, answer in the form asked

Work out a mean, median, mode, range, percentage, percentage change, ratio or fraction from a small table, sometimes to a set number of decimal places or significant figures.

Command word: CalculateUsually 1-2 marksAbout 1 minute per mark

What Pearson asks

Work out a mean, median, mode, range, percentage, percentage change, ratio or fraction from a small table, sometimes to a set number of decimal places or significant figures.

How the marks are earned

Usually one mark for the correct method or interim figure and one for the final answer in the requested form; one-mark parts need only the correct answer.

Method

  1. Underline the exact statistic and the group or column asked for.

    Why: Using the wrong column is the quickest way to lose every mark.

  2. Write the calculation out in full before using the calculator.

    Why: Working can earn the method mark even if the final figure slips.

  3. Check the rounding instruction (decimal places, significant figures).

    Why: A separate mark often depends on the form of the answer.

  4. Sense-check: is the mean inside the range of the data, is the percentage below 100 for a part of a whole?

    Why: Catches keying errors.

Answer frame

Calculation: [numbers and operation] = [unrounded value]. Answer: [rounded value with unit or %].

Common errors

Inferred, not measured: Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

  • Dividing by the wrong number of values for a mean.
  • For percentage change, dividing by the new value instead of the original.
  • Not rounding, or rounding to the wrong number of places.
  • Giving the median without first putting values in order.

Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Add the 55 year olds' scores: 21 + 26 + 35 + 34 + 27 + 28 + 16 = 187. Divide by the 7 participants: 187 / 7 = 26.714... Mean = 26.71 (2 d.p.)

Why it earns the marks

The correct total over the right number of participants earns the calculation mark, and giving 26.71 meets the two-decimal-place instruction for the second mark.
