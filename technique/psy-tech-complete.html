Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Psychology · 1PS0

Complete or draw: tables, graphs and diagrams

Finish a bar chart or scatter diagram from table data (including the y-axis label and scale), or fill missing labels in a diagram or flow model.

Command words: Complete, DrawUsually 1-3 marksAbout 1-1.5 minutes per mark

What Pearson asks

Finish a bar chart or scatter diagram from table data (including the y-axis label and scale), or fill missing labels in a diagram or flow model.

How the marks are earned

For graphs, one mark for a correct y-axis label and scale and one for each correctly plotted bar or point; for diagrams, one mark per correct label.

Method

  1. Find the exact rows or condition you need to plot in the table.

    Why: Only the requested data earns marks.

  2. Label the y-axis with what is measured and choose an even scale that fits the largest value.

    Why: The label and scale are a separate mark.

  3. Plot with a ruler and sharp pencil, matching the style of bars already drawn.

    Why: Bars or points must be accurate to the scale.

  4. For diagrams, use the precise technical term.

    Why: Vague labels are not credited.

Answer frame

Y-axis: '[number/score of ...]' with a 0-[max] scale in equal steps; plot each required value; label diagram parts with specific terms.

Common errors

Inferred, not measured: Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

  • Missing or vague y-axis label.
  • Uneven scale steps.
  • Plotting the wrong condition or participant.
  • Using an everyday word instead of the technical term on a diagram.
