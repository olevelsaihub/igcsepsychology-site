    *19 A teacher told his students that they were going to have a test in two weeks’ time on their most recent history topic. He said that this was a good chance to demonstrate how hard they had worked. In the next lesson, the teacher was called out of the classroom and the students at the front of the classroom saw the test questions and example answers on the teacher’s desk. They took a photograph on their phones of the questions and answers and sent them to their history group chat. Some of the students read the questions and used the answers to cheat in the test. The rest of the students deleted the chat message and did not look at the questions or answers. Assess how well Vohs and Schooler (2008) can explain the behaviour of the students.
