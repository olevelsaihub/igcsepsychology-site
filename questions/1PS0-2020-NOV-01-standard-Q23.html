    *23 Petra is visiting the local park with her mother. Whilst at the park, Petra sees a dog and becomes scared. Petra’s mother tells her that the dog will not harm her and it is a pet like the cat they have at home. Petra sees other children stroking the dog and her mother tells her to stroke the dog too. Petra is anxious and nervous but strokes the dog. Assess Petra’s behaviour using two areas of psychology that you have studied. .
