    *19 Sammie is 10 years old and in the fifth grade at school. Her mother, Joanna, notices that she has few friends at school and always says that the girls in her class are more intelligent than her. Sammie does not like being away from her mother for long. Joanna is worried because Sammie is starting a new school in September. She does not think Sammie will cope. Joanna reads an article about the benefits of owning a pet, so buys Sammie a rabbit. After a few months looking after her rabbit, Sammie seems more self-confident. She recently went to play at a friend’s house for the very first time. Assess how well Van Houtte and Jarvis (1995) can explain Sammie’s behaviour.
