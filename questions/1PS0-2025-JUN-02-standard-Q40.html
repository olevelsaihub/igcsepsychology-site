    *40 Tyler is a lecturer at Dunarvon University. He is hoping to arrange a meeting with Zhang, a newly appointed lecturer who has recently arrived in the country. Tyler emails Zhang suggesting a meeting at three in the afternoon. He tells Zhang that he is happy to move the meeting forwards or backwards if this time is not convenient. Zhang replies that he would like the meeting to be moved above three in the afternoon if possible. Tyler is confused about what time Zhang wants the meeting to take place. Assess how well linguistic relativism can account for Tyler’s lack of understanding about what time to arrange the meeting for.
