    *26 Kim was walking through the park. She noticed the leaves on the trees were turning brown and that there were not very many flowers. Kim passed a small pond where there were ducks and swans moving away from the edge of the pond. In the distance Kim could see a river and horses. As Kim walked down a path she saw two people running towards her wearing sports clothes. She continued to walk without being worried about the strangers running towards her. Assess whether the Direct Theory of Perception (Gibson, 1966) can explain how Kim was able to interpret her surroundings.
